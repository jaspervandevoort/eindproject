    Enforce ticket resale requirements and admin-only organizer creation
    
    Organizer creation: enforce required name/email/password and validate
    the optional validationUrl's format when present.
    
    Ticket resale flow:
    - Reject tickets for unregistered events and asking prices above the
      event's original price (was previously unchecked).
    - Derive the seller from the JWT instead of trusting a client-supplied
      ownerId.
    - New tickets are immediately for sale, matching the "offer a ticket
      for sale" story (no separate listing step in these requirements).
    - Enforce ownership and "not sold / not deleted" checks on price
      updates (previously missing entirely) and compare the new price
      against the original ticket price rather than the current asking
      price.
    - Enforce the same "not sold" check on ticket deletion.
    - Replace generic 500-causing RuntimeExceptions with proper
      @ResponseStatus exceptions (404/409/400) for these rejection cases.
